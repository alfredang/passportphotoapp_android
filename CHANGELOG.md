## [1.0.0] — 2026-07-06 · versionCode 1

### Added
- One-tap selfie capture with a front-camera view and a head-positioning oval guide.
- Automatic background removal using on-device ML Kit selfie segmentation.
- Pure white background compositing that meets passport / visa requirements.
- Face-aware cropping that sizes and positions your head to official specifications.
- Per-country passport sizes (Singapore default) — SG, US, UK, EU/Schengen, AU, CA, IN, CN, JP, MY.
- 300 DPI print-ready output at the exact pixel dimensions each authority expects.
- Save to Gallery and Share.
- Feedback tab (WhatsApp) and About tab.
- 100% on-device processing — no photos are ever uploaded.
