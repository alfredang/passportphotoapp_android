## Full description (≤4000 chars)
Turn a selfie into a clean, compliant passport photo — background removed, distractions filtered out, and cropped to an official size. Everything is processed 100% on your device: no uploads, no accounts, no data collection.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Take a selfie (an oval guide helps you position your head) or pick a photo from your library.
2. The app removes the background and composites you onto a pure white backdrop.
3. Your face is detected and the photo is cropped so your head size and eye line meet official requirements.
4. Save the finished photo to your gallery or share it — print-ready at 300 DPI.

SUPPORTED COUNTRY SIZES
• Singapore (default) — 35 × 45 mm
• United States — 2 × 2 in
• United Kingdom — 35 × 45 mm
• EU / Schengen — 35 × 45 mm
• Australia — 35 × 45 mm
• Canada — 50 × 70 mm
• India — 35 × 45 mm
• China — 33 × 48 mm
• Japan — 35 × 45 mm
• Malaysia — 35 × 50 mm

PRIVACY FIRST
Your photos never leave your device. Capture, background removal, face detection, cropping, and export all happen locally. The app has no server, no ads, and no analytics.

Note: always check your issuing authority's current photo requirements before submitting an application. This app helps you produce a correctly sized photo but cannot guarantee acceptance.
