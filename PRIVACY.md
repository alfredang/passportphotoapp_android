# Privacy Policy — Passport Photo (Android)

_Last updated: 6 July 2026_

**Passport Photo** ("the app"), developed by **Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte. Ltd.**, is designed to be private by default.

## Data collection

The app collects **no data**. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

## Photo processing

Every photo you capture or select is processed **entirely on your device**:

- Selfie capture uses your device camera locally.
- Background removal and face detection run on-device using Google ML Kit models bundled with the app.
- Cropping and export happen locally.

**No image ever leaves your device.** The app has no server and makes no network calls with your content.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — used only to take your selfie inside the app. Optional: you can instead pick a photo from your library via the system photo picker (which shares only the photo you choose).
- **Storage (Android 9 and below only)** — used only to save your finished passport photo to the gallery when you tap Save.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the finished photo is handed to the app you choose via the standard Android share sheet. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
